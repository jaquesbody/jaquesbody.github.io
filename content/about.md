Twenty years of hands-on experience in operations, events, production and logistics. Nine of those running my own drinks business. Now I'm a builder (the actual construction industry) and vibecoder.

I build walls and privacy-first tools for non-tech people and every day use.

Curiosity, optimism and simplicity drive me on. Critical thinking, practical solutions, the odd trowel and personal privacy is how I choose to work.

From depending entirely on external SaaS tools, trial and error and search engines. Now I just build it and learn.

**Experiment. Own my decisions. Iterate. Create.**

I am fully aware of my limitations. I am not a front or backend developer. I am a novice vibe-coder working in my spare time. I get the logic, the possibilities of human creativity coupled with AI. It is not a free ride. I am crucial. AI is brilliant. We are both prone to errors. And here lies my value: in how I can wonder, explore and create.

I love being a novice with limited hardware (8GB of RAM can go far) as my output will only get better. Continuously wanting to learn, accountability and having fun in the process is the way.

Technical note: everything you see has been vibe-coded using free tier AI: Brave Leo (early days), Claude, ChatGPT and OpenCode let loose on Omarchy. No GPU beyond a laptop, an Android phone and a Raspberry Pi 5. Just the right problems.

Just wait.
